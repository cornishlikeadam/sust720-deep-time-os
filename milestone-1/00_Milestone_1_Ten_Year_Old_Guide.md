# Milestone 1: The 10-Year-Old Explorer's Field Guide 🗺️✨
**Welcome, Explorer!**  
Think of this guide as your secret decoder ring. Before anyone builds an awesome project or uses a smart robot (AI), they have to make a **Course Map** and a **Stakeholder Snapshot**. 

Here is what that actually means, how it works, and how you can fill in your own templates in 10 minutes.

---

## Part 1: What is a "Course Map"? 🗺️
Imagine your class is going on a 5-step quest into a giant forest:
1. You start at the trailhead (Step 1).
2. You climb a steep mountain (Step 2).
3. You build a treehouse (Step 3).
4. Your friends test if the treehouse is strong (Step 4).
5. The Park Ranger grades your treehouse and gives you a badge (Step 5).

A **Course Map** simply draws every step of that journey so nobody gets lost, nobody gets hurt, and everyone knows who is in charge of what.

---

## Part 2: The 8 Secret Ingredients 🔍
*(These come straight from the teacher's secret notebook!)*

1. 🎯 **The Goal Sits at the Very Top:**  
   Write ONE sentence describing what the students will actually **make or do** with their own hands.  
   *Bad goal:* "Students understand climate change." (You can't see understanding in someone's head!)  
   *Good goal:* "Students construct a 500-year anti-fragile ecological blueprint and defend a generational ethics charter." (Observable! Testable!)

2. 👣 **Real Steps in Order:**  
   Four or five real boxes showing what happens week by week. No fancy mysterious names—just real actions like *Draw*, *Stress-Test*, *Build*, *Critique*, *Grade*.

3. 👥 **Real People Named:**  
   Write down EVERY person who touches the project: the student making it, the teacher grading it, and even the classmates giving feedback!

4. 🚦 **Big Choices, Not Just Chores:**  
   A step isn't just "do homework." It has to answer: **"Who decides here?"** and **"What does their choice change next?"** If the choice doesn't change anything, it's just busywork.

5. 🔬 **Proof it Works (Evidence Attached to a Step):**  
   Don't just say "students get better." Point to a specific step and say: *"Students fixing their drawing errors across 3 weeks proves the teacher's feedback worked."*

6. 🤖 **The Helper Robot in a Box (AI Insertion Point):**  
   Pick ONE small, low-stakes spot where the robot is allowed to help carry the heavy rocks (like brainstorming weird future weather shocks). It must be **labeled**, **ungraded**, and **rejectable** (meaning the student can say *"No thanks, robot, that idea is silly"*).

7. 🛑 **The Red Line (Non-Use Boundary):**  
   Draw a thick red line where robots are **forbidden**. For example: a robot can NEVER grade a student's heart, and a robot can NEVER steal sacred stories from Indigenous elders. The boundary protects human judgment!

8. 👑 **The King/Queen Human Decision (Preserved Judgment):**  
   Name the human being who gets the final say. No matter what the robot calculates, the **human student** chooses what to build, and the **human teacher** decides what is ready to pass.

## Part 3: What is a "Stakeholder Snapshot"? 🪑
When you throw a big party, who comes? And who gets stuck cleaning the kitchen?  
The **Stakeholder Snapshot** pulls up **4 Chairs at the Table**:

* 🎮 **Chair 1: The Players (The Users):**  
  The students doing the assignment. They want to learn cool skills, but they risk letting the robot do all their thinking for them.
* 👨‍🏫 **Chair 2: The Bosses (Decision Makers):**  
  The professors and school directors. They want high-quality work, but they worry about robots tricking them.
* 🌲 **Chair 3: The Neighbors (Affected People):**  
  People who didn't take the class, but will feel the effects of what students design—like future clients, community elders, and the environment.
* 🔌 **Chair 4: The Invisible Helpers (Burdened People):**  
  The people or things carrying the hidden weight that nobody talks about: data center workers in other countries, rivers drained to cool supercomputers, and kids born 500 years in the future!

---

## Part 4: The Missing-Person Detective Game 🕵️
This is the most important part of the whole project! Look at your party list and ask:  
**"Who did we forget to invite?"**

In the teacher's example, they forgot **Denise the adjunct teacher**. Nobody paid her to learn the new computer program, but she had to show up Monday morning and answer questions about it!

In our Deep Time sustainability course, people often forget:
1. **The River and the Marsh:** The water being sucked into giant computer servers to run AI.
2. **The 7th-Generation Grandchildren:** The kids living in the year 2526 who have to live inside the world we design today!

Whenever you find a Missing Person, you fill out the **Secret Detective Card**:
* 💬 **The Gap, in Their Words:** A quote in their own voice explaining what hurts or what is unfair.
* 🏷️ **Who They Are:** Their real name, role, and why they were left out.
* 🛠️ **What Would Change It:** A concrete rule or safeguard to fix the problem right now!
