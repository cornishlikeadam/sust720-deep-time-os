## ✅ Element 5: The 8-Element Scorecard (Self-Audit)
*Check off each element before sharing your map:*
- [ ] **1. Learning Goal at Top:** Is it one sentence? Is it observable and testable (not "understand")?
- [ ] **2. Activities in Order:** Are there real discrete steps instead of vague phase names?
- [ ] **3. Roles Named:** Is every human who touches the work explicitly listed (students, faculty, peers)?
- [ ] **4. Decisions, Not Tasks:** Does each step name who decides, and do arrows show what changes next?
- [ ] **5. Evidence Attached:** Is evidence tied to a specific step to prove learning happened?
- [ ] **6. AI Insertion Point:** Is it placed for a clear pedagogical reason? Is it low-stakes and rejectable?
- [ ] **7. Non-Use Boundary:** Is there a defended boundary where AI is strictly prohibited?
- [ ] **8. Preserved Judgment:** Does the final consequential decision stay 100% human?
