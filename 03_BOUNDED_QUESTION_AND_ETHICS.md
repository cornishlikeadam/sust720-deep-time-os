## 3. The Five Ethical Questions Evaluation

1. **Q1: Who benefits and who bears the burden?**
   - *Analysis:* Students expand combinatorial capacity to model multi-century cascading shocks. The burden is borne by data center water tables and global data workers. **[Evidence]**
   - *Safeguard:* Strict 5-token query quota per team; local small models prioritized. **[Test]**
2. **Q2: Does this displace human relational judgment?**
   - *Analysis:* If AI selects the 'optimal' sustainable system, human agency is usurped. Restricting AI to raw climate stress-testing keeps design agency with humans. **[Assumption]**
   - *Safeguard:* Trace Test verifies that 100% of regenerative architectures are human-authored. **[Test]**
3. **Q3: Does this respect Indigenous Data Sovereignty?**
   - *Analysis:* Commercial LLMs ingest sacred oral knowledge without consent (violating CARE/OCAP). **[Evidence]**
   - *Safeguard:* Hard Non-Use Boundary in Step 1. Direct analog study of Indigenous-authored texts only. **[Test]**
4. **Q4: Is the decision logic transparent and inspectable?**
   - *Analysis:* Black-box neural models cannot explain why a specific biophysical tipping point was triggered. **[Evidence]**
   - *Safeguard:* All model outputs treated as speculative hypotheses requiring 2 empirical paper citations. **[Test]**
5. **Q5: What irreversible harm could occur?**
   - *Analysis:* Students might graduate believing long-range ecological stewardship can be automated, leading to real-world socio-ecological collapse. **[Assumption]**
   - *Safeguard:* Mandatory signed Generational Ethics Charter and oral defense before faculty. **[Test]**
