# Chapter 0: What on Earth is "Deep Time"?
### *The Acorn, The Clock, and the 500-Year Secret*

Imagine you found a clock that doesn't tick every second.  
Instead, it ticks **once every human lifetime**—about once every 80 years! ⏳

Most people live their lives on the fast clock:
- *“Hurry up, eat your breakfast in 10 minutes!”*
- *“School starts in 5 minutes!”*
- *“What’s for dinner tonight?”*
- *“How much money can a company make in the next 3 months?”*

This fast clock makes people build things quickly, buy plastic stuff that breaks tomorrow, and forget about what happens to the trash.

```

```

### The Deep Time Clock
**Deep Time** is when you slow down and look at the world through the eyes of a rock, an ancient redwood tree, or a river. 

In our class, **SUST 720: Designing in Deep Time**, we decided to design something for the year **2526 CE**. That is **500 years from today!**

Think about who will live in 2526:
- Your great-great-great-great-great-great-great-great-great grandchildren!
- Migratory birds who need clean air.
- Dolphins and marsh grasses in the salt marshes of Savannah, Georgia.

If we want the earth to be happy and healthy 500 years from now, we can't just build normal things. We have to design things that are **ANTI-FRAGILE**.

### What does "Anti-Fragile" mean?
- A **fragile** coffee cup shatters if you drop it on the floor. 💥
- A **resilient** rubber ball bounces back, but stays the same. 🎾
- An **anti-fragile** living creature gets *stronger* when faced with surprises and challenges—just like your muscles get stronger when you run and climb trees! 🌳💪

In our course, we want to design sustainable systems for Savannah that get *better* even when giant storms, sea level rise, and heatwaves happen over 500 years.
