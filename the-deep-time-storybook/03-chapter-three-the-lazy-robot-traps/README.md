# Chapter 3: Watch Out for the Lazy Robot Traps!

### The Big Classroom Battle: Expelling the Robot!
In our first draft, we thought: *“Hey, maybe the AI can help us write our design report in Step 3!”*

Our director stopped us right there and said:
> **“NO! If you let the AI design your sustainable system in Step 3, you trigger Card 5 (Brain Muscle Atrophy) and Card 6 (The Rubber Stamp Trap)! You won't be sustainable designers anymore—you'll just be babysitters for a bad television script!”**

So we made a **Big Structural Change**:
- We **kicked the AI completely out of Step 3!**
- Human students do 100% of the thinking, drawing, and designing.
- The AI was locked in a cage in **Step 2**, only allowed to act like unexpected weather throwing surprises at us.
- And to fix Card 4 (The Thirsty Computers), each team was given **only 5 computer tokens** for the whole semester! When your 5 tokens are gone, you cannot use the AI anymore.
