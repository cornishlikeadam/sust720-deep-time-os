## 🌟 Welcome to the Storybook!

Have you ever planted an acorn in the dirt? 🌰

If you water it, you won't get a giant tree by dinner time. You won't even get a giant tree by your next birthday! It takes **hundreds of years** for an acorn to become a mighty oak tree where birds build nests, squirrels hide acorns, and children play in the shade.

In a special design school called **SCAD (Savannah College of Art and Design)**, grown-up students in a class called **SUST 720: Designing in Deep Time** are learning how to be **"Good Ancestors."** 

Instead of just planning for tomorrow or next week, they are planning **500 YEARS into the future**—all the way to the year **2526!** 🚀

And because they are using super-smart computer helpers called **AI**, they had to build strict rules so the computers don't ruin the magic or do all their homework for them.

This book walks you through the entire project, step-by-step, just like walking through the golden halls of a grand palace!
