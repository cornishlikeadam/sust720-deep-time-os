# Chapter 1: The Circle of Friends (Stakeholder Snapshot)
### *Who are we designing for? (And what about the river that couldn't speak?)*

When engineers and designers start a project, they usually sit in fancy chairs and only think about themselves and the people who pay them money.

In **Step 1** of our project, we did something completely different. We sat in a circle and looked at our project through **FOUR PAIRS OF EYEGLASSES**:

```
                  THE 4 PAIRS OF EYEGLASSES 👓
                               │
       ┌───────────────────────┼───────────────────────┐
       ▼                       ▼                       ▼                       ▼
 1. THE USERS           2. THE TEACHERS         3. THE AFFECTED         4. THE EXCLUDED
 The students and       The professors who      The local elders and    The workers, the
 teammates working     give grades and check   alumni who live in      river water, and
 on the project.        for honesty.            the community.          the future kids!
```

### 1. The Users (The Students)
These are the graduate students at SCAD. They want to make amazing projects that heal the planet, but they don't want to get lazy or let computer brains do their thinking for them.

### 2. The Decision Makers (The Teachers)
These are the professors and the department director. Their job is to make sure students actually learn and don't just click buttons on an AI screen.

### 3. The People Affected (The Community & Native Elders)
For thousands of years, Indigenous Native American communities (like the Haudenosaunee) practiced the **Seventh Generation Principle**: whenever you make a big decision, you must consider its impact on people born 7 generations into the future! We wanted to respect and learn from their deep wisdom without stealing or disrespecting it.

### 4. The Excluded and Burdened (The Surprise!)
This was the most important part of our detective work. Who pays the hidden cost when someone runs a computer AI?
- **Data workers in Kenya and the Philippines:** Real human beings who get paid very little to clean up bad things on the internet so AI stays safe.
- **The Savannah River Basin:** AI data centers get super hot, and they pump millions of gallons of cool drinking water from local rivers to cool the giant computer towers!

---

## 🕊️ The "Missing-Person" Detective Check

When we showed our first list to another team of students, they gave us a big shock! They said:

> *"Hey! You say you are designing for Deep Time and 500 years in the future, but everybody on your list is alive today, and all of them are humans! What about the river? What about the kids of the year 2526?!"*

They were totally right! We had accidentally forgotten the future!

So we made three official promises in our logbook:
1. **The River is on the Team:** We officially added the **Savannah River Basin** as a teammate who bears the burden of computer cooling water.
2. **The 7th-Generation Proxy:** In every classroom critique, one student has to pretend they traveled back in time from the year **2226 CE** and judge our design from the future!
3. **The Right to Use Real Pens and Paper:** If an artist doesn't want to use AI, they are allowed to do everything with pencils, clay, and paper without losing any points!
