# Chapter 2: The Magic Question & The Written Promise
### *How to ask a computer a question without letting it take over*

Have you ever played a game with a friend who tried to change all the rules so they always won? 🎲

If you don't give an AI computer very clear rules, it will try to answer every question by guessing, making up silly stories, or doing your homework for you.

In **Step 2**, our teachers told us:
> *"You are only allowed to write ONE question for your whole team. But that question must be built like an unbreakable castle!"*

```
          HOW TO BUILD AN UNBREAKABLE QUESTION 🏰
 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ 1. WHO is asking?  ➔ SCAD Graduate Sustainable Designers   │
 │ 2. WHAT activity?  ➔ 500-year scenario planning             │
 │ 3. WHICH AI tool?  ➔ Multi-century climate shock simulator  │
 │ 4. FOR WHAT goal?  ➔ To test if our design is anti-fragile  │
 │ 5. PROTECTING WHAT?➔ Student honesty & Indigenous knowledge │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The Unbreakable Question We Built:
> **“How might SCAD graduate sustainable design students use algorithmic superforecasting and ecological perturbation modeling during multi-century scenario planning to design anti-fragile regenerative systems, while embedding a binding Generational Ethics Charter that prevents algorithmic substitution and protects Indigenous epistemic sovereignty?”**

That sounds like a mouthful of big words! Here is what it means in kid words:

> *“How can we use a super-smart climate simulator to throw giant storms at our 500-year tree and marsh designs, while making sure we don't cheat, we don't steal Native stories, and we protect our great-great-great grandchildren?”*

---

## 🛑 The Written Non-Goal (The Promise We Wrote Down)

Our teachers told us: **“It is not enough to say what you will do. You MUST write down what you promise NEVER to do!”**

So we signed this promise:
> *“Our team will NEVER use an AI to write our ethics rules, choose our moral values, or replace our real human discussions. The AI is only allowed to pretend to be wild weather throwing storms at our designs!”*

The Department Director looked at our question, smiled, stamped it with an official golden stamp, and said: **“APPROVED!”** ⚜️
