# Chapter 5: The Secret Detective Test (Peer Trace)
### *Can your friends follow your map without you saying a single word?*

Have you ever drawn a treasure map for your friends? 🗺️🏴‍☠️

If your treasure map has messy squiggles, missing trees, and confusing arrows, your friends will get lost in the bushes.

In **Step 5**, our class did the **Peer Trace Test**.  
The rule was simple and terrifying:
1. You take your whole course map.
2. You hand it to a different team of students (Team 02: The Bio-Regional Futures Studio).
3. **YOU ARE NOT ALLOWED TO TALK!** You have to sit quietly with your hands folded while they try to solve your map!

```
                  THE 5-POINT DETECTIVE CHECKLIST 🕵️‍♂️
 [x] 1. Can they follow the path from Step 1 all the way to Step 5?
 [x] 2. Can they name who makes the big choices at every step?
 [x] 3. Can they spot the exact yellow circle where the AI helps?
 [x] 4. Can they spot the red shield where AI is FORBIDDEN?
 [x] 5. Can they spot the purple crown where ONLY HUMANS make choices?
```

### The Detective Report from Team 02
Team 02 took our map, put on their detective hats, and tested all five points.

- They found our yellow circle! 🟡
- They found our red shields! 🔴
- They followed our path from the year 2026 to 2526! 🚀

**Result:** **ALL 5 DETECTIVE CHECKS PASSED!**  
Because our map was so clear and honest, anybody—even someone who wasn't in our team—could see who was in charge and where the computer was kept on a leash.
