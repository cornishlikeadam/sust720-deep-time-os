### The Three-Stage Friction Review Path
1. **Stage 1 (Empirical Cross-Examination):** Student verifies the biophysical plausibility of the AI-generated climate stressor against two peer-reviewed ecological papers.
2. **Stage 2 (Relational Consequence Statement):** Student writes a 100-word analysis: *"Who suffers? Which more-than-human species are displaced? How does this affect human communities in 2226 CE?"*
3. **Stage 3 (Oral Defense Gate):** Student defends their system's anti-fragile response to faculty during desk critique before integrating it into the master blueprint.
