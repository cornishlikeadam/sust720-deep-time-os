## System Overview

This repository houses the **Course Editor and AI Governance Operating System** for **SUST 720: Designing in Deep Time**. The course equips graduate sustainable designers with methodologies in superforecasting, anti-fragility, worldmaking, and transformative scenario planning, engaging speculative design, design fiction, and Indigenous ways of being and knowing across extended multi-century horizons (50 to 500+ years).

The entire course activity is structured around developing **ONE comprehensive deliverable**:
> ### The Deep-Time Anti-Fragility Blueprint & Generational Ethics Charter
> 1. **500-Year Bioregional System Architecture:** Regenerative infrastructure, material circularity, and ecological succession.
> 2. **Algorithmic Superforecasting Integration:** Rigorous application of AI to model multi-century climate volatility and tipping points.
> 3. **The Generational Ethics Charter:** A binding covenant defining the Assistance Line, Non-Use Boundaries, and moral accountability to seven generations of unborn descendants.
