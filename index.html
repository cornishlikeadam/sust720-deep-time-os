What is the Transparency Log? (Definition & Purpose)

Core Instrument of the SCAD AI Governance Framework

The Transparency Log is the official, verifiable audit ledger of a graduate design team’s ethical deliberations. Mandated under the SCAD AI Governance Framework (September 17, 2026), it is not a casual changelog or note repository—it is the constitutional proof of human accountability.

🛡️

1. What Does It Do?

It records every consequential decision where human agency, AI capability, and ethical boundaries intersect. It documents who decided, what information was inspected, what trade-offs were confronted, and what values were protected.

⚡

2. Why Does It Matter?

Without a transparency log, AI use collapses into covert substitution: students use AI, claim it was 'assistance', and nobody can verify where the machine ended and the human began. The log makes the human review path inspectable and falsifiable.

🕊️

3. The Missing-Person Check

The log permanently records who was omitted from initial design drafts (e.g., non-human river biomes, unborn 7th-generation descendants, exploited data workers) and tracks the concrete structural revisions made to address those gaps.

🏛️

4. Accreditation & Evaluation

During the Director Check and final studio juries, faculty and NASAD accreditors inspect the Transparency Log to certify that graduate learning outcomes were achieved through authentic human cognition rather than algorithmic mimicry.
