## 🏛️ The One Core Deliverable

The entire course activity is organized around producing **ONE master deliverable with ethics and applied AI**:

> ### 📜 The Capstone Deliverable:
> **The Deep-Time Anti-Fragility Blueprint & Generational Ethics Charter**  
> *(Le Grand Devis d'Anti-Fragilité Écologique et Charte Éthique Séculaire)*

1. **500-Year Bioregional System Architecture:** Regenerative infrastructure, living materials, circular hydrological loops, and soil microbiology for coastal Georgia salt marshes.
2. **Algorithmic Superforecasting Integration:** AI applied strictly to simulate external non-linear biophysical climate shocks across Year +50 to +500.
3. **The Generational Ethics Charter:** A binding covenant applying the **Assistance Line** and **CARE/OCAP** Indigenous Data Sovereignty principles across seven generations of unborn descendants.
