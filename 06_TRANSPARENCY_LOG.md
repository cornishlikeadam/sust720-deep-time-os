## 1. What is the Transparency Log?

The **Transparency Log** is the foundational governance instrument of the SCAD AI Governance Framework. It is a permanent, inspectable chronological audit trail that documents every consequential design choice where human judgment, artificial intelligence capabilities, and ethical boundaries meet.

### The Four Vital Functions of the Log:
1. **Preventing Covert Substitution:**  
   Without an explicit transparency log, AI use collapses into covert substitution: students use AI, claim it was merely "assistance", and neither faculty nor peers can inspect where algorithmic generation ended and authentic human critical reasoning began. The log creates an inspectable review path.
2. **Documenting Boundary Movements:**  
   Whenever an AI insertion point is added, modified, or evicted—such as when the Director Critique mandated evicting AI from Step 3 system architecture—the log records the rationale, the date, and the actor responsible.
3. **Recording Missing-Person Discoveries:**  
   During Step 1 peer audits, teams actively test who was left out of the stakeholder snapshot. The log records the omission and details the concrete structural changes enacted to close the gap (e.g., adding the Savannah River Basin and a 7th-Generation Peer Proxy).
4. **Providing Falsifiable Evidence for Accreditation:**  
   During the Director Check and final studio juries, faculty and NASAD evaluators inspect the Transparency Log to certify that graduate learning outcomes were achieved through authentic human cognition rather than automated regurgitation.
