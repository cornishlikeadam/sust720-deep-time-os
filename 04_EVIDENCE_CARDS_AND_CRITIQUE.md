### Deep Dive into Evidence Cards:
1. **Card 01 (Epistemic Flattening):** LLMs trained primarily on digitized English internet text prioritize Eurocentric, progressive, anthropocentric timelines. When asked about time, they default to progress narratives (technology solving nature) rather than cyclical or kin-centric temporalities (Kimmerer 2013).
2. **Card 02 (Temporal Anchoring):** Speculative prompts generate homogenized aesthetics (e.g., sleek sci-fi glass towers with trees or dystopian Mad Max deserts), stifling students' capacity for authentic design fiction (Dunne & Raby 2013).
3. **Card 03 (Ghost Labor):** Every model query relies on the unseen, undercompensated labor of thousands of content moderation workers in Kenya, India, and the Philippines (Gray & Suri 2019).
4. **Card 04 (Ecological Cost of Inference):** Training and querying frontier models consumes vast amounts of potable freshwater for cooling and relies on fossil-fueled electric grids (Luccioni et al. 2023).
5. **Card 05 (Cognitive Atrophy):** Students who use AI to generate scenarios demonstrate decreased ability to engage in primary literature synthesis and lose tolerance for ambiguity (Carr 2020).
6. **Card 06 (The Rubber-Stamp Oversight Illusion):** When systems prompt humans to "review and approve" AI outputs, human attention degrades within 3 iterations, resulting in 92% passive acceptance without critical verification (Cummings 2017).

## 3. Peer & Director Critique: Structural Revisions

**Critique Session:** September 17, 2026  
**Critique Panel:** SCAD Faculty Director + Team 01 (Coastal Ecology Studio)

### Critique Feedback Received:
> *"Your initial proposal had AI inserted into Step 3 to help students design the core regenerative system and write the ethics charter. That directly triggers Evidence Card 05 (Cognitive Atrophy) and Card 06 (Rubber-Stamp Oversight). If the AI designs the solution or drafts the ethics, the graduate student is no longer practicing sustainable systems thinking; they are just rubber-stamping algorithmic defaults."*

### Mandatory Structural Revisions Enacted:
1. **Eviction of AI from Step 3:** We completely removed the AI insertion point from Step 3 (Regenerative System Design & AI Governance). Step 3 is now a **Preserve-Human-Judgment Zone**.
2. **Hard Isolation of AI to Step 2 Stress-Testing:** The AI insertion point is restricted exclusively to **Step 2 (Scenario Branching)**, where it acts strictly as an environmental antagonist (generating unpredicted ecological perturbations), while all regenerative system architecture, material selection, and ethical covenants remain 100% human-authored.
3. **Introduction of Query Quotas:** To address Evidence Card 04 (Ecological Impact), each student team is issued a physical "Compute Token Card" permitting only 5 model queries for the entire project, forcing intentional, high-stakes prompt formulation.
